The pastoral visibility problem

Behaviour information often sits in too many places.

Schools often manage behaviour concerns through emails, spreadsheets, teacher notes, parent call logs, meeting records, and separate pastoral documents. This can make it difficult to see patterns, coordinate support, and ensure follow-ups are completed. TrackEDU helps bring these workflows into a clearer shared system.

When behaviour tracking is manual

Important context can become fragmented.

  • Behaviour notes may be stored across email, spreadsheets, paper forms, or teacher notebooks.
  • Pastoral teams may not see repeated concerns until they have already escalated.
  • Parent communication can be difficult to trace if it is recorded inconsistently.
  • Teachers and counsellors may duplicate work because follow-up ownership is unclear.
  • School leaders may lack timely visibility into student support needs.

With TrackEDU Behavior Tracker

Behaviour records become easier to review and act on.

  • Teachers can log behaviour concerns through one connected school system.
  • Pastoral teams can monitor tiered interventions and follow-up actions.
  • Parent communication logs can be connected to the student support record.
  • School leaders can review patterns by student, class, grade level, or support need.
  • Schools can export relevant data to CSV for review and reporting.

Questions about behaviour tracking software

What is behaviour tracking software?

Behaviour tracking software helps schools record incidents, identify patterns, manage interventions, document parent communication, and support pastoral teams with clearer student information.

Who can use TrackEDU's Behavior Tracker?

Depending on school access permissions, the Behavior Tracker can support teachers, pastoral teams, counsellors, school leaders, and administrators.

Does TrackEDU support tiered interventions?

Yes. TrackEDU supports tiered intervention tracking, helping schools move from early classroom concerns through pastoral support and leadership-level follow-up when needed.

Can TrackEDU record parent communication?

Yes. TrackEDU includes parent communication logs so schools can keep contact records connected to student behaviour and support workflows.

Can schools try TrackEDU before buying?
