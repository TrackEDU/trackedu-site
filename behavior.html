How it works

How the school behavior tracker works

The system is designed so staff can understand what to do without learning a complicated workflow.

1. Log the incident

Choose the student, behaviour, severity and action taken. Add a brief factual note.

2. Track the intervention

The student profile shows their current tier, points, history and any required intervention steps.

3. Follow up

Record parent contact, complete follow-up tasks and alert the staff who need to know.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this a standalone product?

Yes. TrackEDU Behavior Management is available as a standalone annual school licence.

How does tier progression work?

Schools use a structured Pre-Tier 1 to Tier 3 pathway. Incident severity contributes points, helping staff see when the next intervention level is reached.

Can we use our own behavior categories and rules?

Yes. Administrators can configure behavior categories, actions, notification rules and other school settings.

Who is TrackEDU Behavior designed for?

It is designed for schools that need a shared behavior tracking system for teachers, pastoral staff and school leaders, including incident logging, intervention tracking, parent communication, follow-up tasks and reporting.

What student information should we enter?

Use only information your school considers necessary and lawful. TrackEDU recommends first names or school-approved identifiers where practical. Student photographs are not required or supported.

Can we export the data?

Yes. Schools can export incident records to CSV and filter by student, class, grade or date range.
