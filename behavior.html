FAQ

Common questions

Can we use the Behavior Management system alone?

Yes. It's available as a standalone annual subscription. You can also upgrade to the full TrackEDU platform later.

How does tier progression work?

Each incident has points (Low=0.5, Medium=1, High=2). Once a student reaches the points required for the next tier, it unlocks automatically. Pre-Tier 1 → Tier 1 at 4 points, Tier 1 → Tier 2 at 3 points, etc.

Is the system ICO compliant and secure?

Yes. TrackEDU is ICO registered (Ref: ZC146923) for data protection compliance. We use Google Firebase with enterprise-grade security, data encryption in transit and at rest. Student names are never shown in full to protect privacy — only first name + last initial. Schools retain full ownership of their data.

Can we export all incident data?

Absolutely. You can export CSV files of all incidents, filtered by student, class, grade, or date range.
