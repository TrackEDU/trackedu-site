FAQ

Common questions

Is TrackEDU Behavior a standalone product?

Yes. It is available as a standalone annual school licence for USD 499/year.

How does tier progression work?

Schools can use a structured Pre-Tier 1 to Tier 3 pathway. Incident severity contributes points and the system helps staff see when the next intervention level is reached.

Can we use our own behavior categories?

Yes. Administrators can configure behavior categories, actions, notification rules and other school settings.

Can schools export their records?

Yes. Schools can export incident records to CSV and use year-end leadership reporting for transition planning.

Can we pay by credit card?

Credit card checkout through Stripe is coming soon. Schools can currently request an invoice and pay by bank transfer or purchase order.

Which behavior tracker is the best fit?

There is no single best behavior tool for every school. ClassDojo is attractive when the priority is classroom culture and family communication. LiveSchool is built strongly around positive-behavior systems, points and rewards. PBISApps SWIS is an established choice for schools focused on discipline referral data and PBIS decision-making. TrackEDU Behavior is aimed at schools that want a simple standalone system connecting incidents, tiered support, parent contact, follow-up work, leadership visibility and next-year handover without moving into a much larger school platform.
