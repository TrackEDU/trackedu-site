Questions

Good to know before you start.

Is the trial really 30 days?

Yes. Schools can try TrackEDU free for 30 days. No credit card is needed.

Does Incident Tracker need a separate login?

No. It uses the same TrackEDU school account and login as the teacher app.

Is Incident Tracker included in Complete TrackEDU?

Yes. Incident Tracker is included with the complete school licence, alongside House Points, attendance and other tools.

Can we import our students?

Yes. Google Classroom and CSV import are available. Wonde is also available with a subscription.

Are parent contact details imported?

No. Parent details are not imported. If a school wants to keep them, it can add them by hand.

Does TrackEDU use student photos?

No. TrackEDU does not use student photos.

Can we set up our own support levels?

Schools can set up incident categories and support settings to suit their needs.

Can we export incident records?

Yes. Schools can export incident records to CSV.

How do we pay after the trial?

Schools can request an invoice for the annual licence. Bank transfer and purchase orders are supported.
