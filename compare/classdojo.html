Questions Schools Ask

ClassDojo Alternative FAQs

Clear answers for schools comparing behaviour tracking, house points and student culture platforms.

What makes TrackEDU different from ClassDojo?

ClassDojo is widely associated with individual classroom communication and reward systems. TrackEDU is designed for whole-school culture, combining behaviour tracking, house points, rewards, student portals, teacher tools and leadership analytics in one connected platform.

Is TrackEDU suitable for secondary schools?

Yes. TrackEDU is particularly useful for secondary and K-12 schools that need a more structured way to manage house systems, behaviour logs, pastoral follow-up, positive recognition and school-wide trends.

Does TrackEDU support both positive rewards and behaviour concerns?

Yes. TrackEDU supports positive house points and rewards, while also helping schools record behaviour concerns, identify patterns and respond earlier through more visible pastoral data.

Can students see their progress?

Yes. TrackEDU includes a student portal so rewards are not passive. Students can see points, recognition, badges and house progress in a safer, school-controlled environment.
