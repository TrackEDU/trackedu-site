FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Behaviour Tracking Platforms

Answers to common questions about TrackEDU, LiveSchool, PBIS Rewards and CritterCoin.

Is TrackEDU a good alternative to LiveSchool?

Yes. TrackEDU offers broader whole-school features including house points, student portal, pastoral tier tracking and leadership analytics—making it a strong alternative for schools needing more than rewards-only systems.

Does TrackEDU work with PBIS frameworks?

Yes. TrackEDU supports positive reinforcement, tiered interventions (pre-Tier 1 to Tier 3) and data-driven recognition that aligns with PBIS models while adding house points and student engagement.

How does CritterCoin compare to TrackEDU?

CritterCoin is a simple token economy designed for younger students (K-5). TrackEDU is a whole-school platform for K-12 with house points, secondary-ready student portal, pastoral tracking and leadership dashboards.

Which platform has the best student engagement features?

TrackEDU's Student Portal includes 3D badges, daily streaks, house boosts and student voice polls—offering deeper engagement than LiveSchool, PBIS Rewards or CritterCoin.
