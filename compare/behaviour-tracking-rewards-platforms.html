Where Each Platform Fits

Different Tools, Different Strengths

The strongest comparison is not “good versus bad”. It is about choosing the right fit for your school’s culture, systems and age range.

TrackEDU

Built to connect house points, behaviour tracking, student rewards, student portal engagement, teacher tools and leadership visibility in one platform.

LiveSchool

A schoolwide behaviour and rewards platform with points, rewards, reports, house points and newer student shopping features.

PBIS Rewards

A dedicated PBIS and MTSS management platform with recognition, digital token economy, reports, stores, CICO and house groups.

CritterCoin

A highly gamified rewards and house points system built around custom digital coins, reward stores, house scores and student engagement.

Why Schools Consider TrackEDU

TrackEDU’s Point of Difference

TrackEDU is positioned for schools that want one connected school culture system rather than separate tools for points, behaviour logs, rewards, student engagement and leadership reporting.

  • Integrated workflow: Teacher App, Student Portal and Behavior Tracker designed to work together.
  • House culture: House points, leaderboards and recognition designed as part of everyday school culture.
  • Student visibility: A student-facing portal so rewards are not passive and recognition can be seen by students.
  • Leadership clarity: Behaviour trends, pastoral concerns and positive recognition visible at whole-school level.

The question is not simply which platform has the most features. The better question is which system best supports the culture, routines and decision-making your school is trying to build.

Deep Dive 3

Student Engagement and Portal Experience

Student-facing design matters because rewards work best when recognition is visible, understandable and motivating.

What established platforms already do well

LiveSchool includes student-facing points and rewards experiences, including newer shopping-style reward tools. PBIS Rewards includes student portal and recognition workflows. CritterCoin offers student engagement through digital coins, rewards, pets and house competition features.

What TrackEDU adds to the conversation

TrackEDU’s student portal is positioned as part of a wider school culture system, linking recognition, house progress and behaviour visibility with teacher and leadership tools rather than treating rewards as a separate activity.

Best Fit

Which Platform Might Fit Your School?

This is a practical starting point, not a final procurement recommendation.

Primary schools

CritterCoin and PBIS Rewards may appeal where the priority is a positive, easy-to-understand token economy. TrackEDU may fit if the school also wants whole-school behaviour visibility.

Secondary / K-12 schools

TrackEDU, LiveSchool and PBIS Rewards are all worth reviewing. TrackEDU is especially relevant where house culture, student portal engagement and pastoral tracking need to connect.

House systems

TrackEDU, LiveSchool, PBIS Rewards and CritterCoin all describe house-related or team-based options in different ways. Compare how each handles setup, reporting and student display.

PBIS / MTSS frameworks

PBIS Rewards is purpose-built for PBIS. TrackEDU may suit schools that want PBIS-style recognition alongside house points, teacher tools and broader culture dashboards.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, positive answers for schools comparing behaviour tracking, rewards and house point systems.

Is TrackEDU a good alternative to LiveSchool?

TrackEDU is worth considering for schools comparing schoolwide behaviour and rewards platforms, particularly if they want house points, a student portal, teacher tools and behaviour visibility in one connected system. LiveSchool is also an established schoolwide behaviour and rewards platform, so the best choice depends on your school’s workflow and priorities.

Does TrackEDU work with PBIS-style positive recognition?

Yes. TrackEDU supports positive recognition, house points, rewards and behaviour pattern visibility. PBIS Rewards is a dedicated PBIS and MTSS platform, so schools with formal PBIS requirements should compare the exact workflows, integrations and reporting they need.

How does CritterCoin compare to TrackEDU?

CritterCoin is strong for gamified digital coins, house systems, rewards stores and student engagement. TrackEDU is positioned more broadly as a connected school culture platform that combines house points, rewards, behaviour tracking, student portal engagement and leadership visibility.

Which platform is best for house points?

Several platforms now support house or team-based points in different ways. TrackEDU focuses on house points as part of a wider school culture platform. CritterCoin has a strong house and digital coin model. LiveSchool and PBIS Rewards also describe house point or house group capabilities. Schools should compare how each platform displays house progress, handles student access and supports reporting.
