6.1 Data ownership. All behavior incident data belongs to the school. Schools may export incident reports to CSV at any time.

6.2 Notification system. Incident notifications are sent to designated staff based on severity (Low, Medium, High) and tier level.

6.3 Role-based visibility. Homeroom Teachers see incidents for their students only. Counsellors see school-wide behavior data. School leaders have administrative visibility.
