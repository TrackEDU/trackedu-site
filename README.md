TrackEDU is a complete school culture operating system featuring three integrated tools: the **Teacher App**, **Student Portal**, and **Behavior Tracker**. Built by former educators, it empowers teachers with frictionless classroom tools, engages students with RPG-style gamification, and gives counsellors tiered behavior intervention tracking—all without requiring any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

---

## ✨ Core Ecosystem

TrackEDU operates as a unified platform with three integrated tools. Everything syncs in real‑time.

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher App
A frictionless, PWA‑optimized dashboard for educators to manage their classroom and house system.
- **Instant Point Awarding:** Award or deduct points to individuals, groups, or whole classes in seconds.
- **12+ Classroom Utilities:** Magic Hat, Squad Builder, Focus Timer, Duo Mode, Role Roulette, Quick Quiz (250+ questions), and more.
- **Live Projector Mode:** Auto‑rotating full‑screen display for assemblies showcasing live house standings.
- **Admin Dashboard:** CSV bulk imports, auto‑balancing algorithms, and cross‑classroom point syncing.

### 🚀 Student Portal
A full‑blown gamified web app that students access on their own devices.
- **The Learner's Journey (3D Binders):** Unlock 3D holographic trading cards across 30 tiers.
- **Daily Streaks & Mystery Spins:** Build streaks and unlock bonus points.
- **House Power Boosts (Reactors):** Tap daily to charge your House Reactor. Hit 100% for a "House Blast" bonus.
- **Mystery Word Missions:** Collaborate across classrooms to crack secret codes.
- **Student Voice Polls:** Live, verified voting on school themes and events.

### 📊 Behavior Tracker
A dedicated tool for counsellors and school leaders to manage student interventions.
- **Tiered Tracking (Pre‑Tier 1 to Tier 3):** Automatic progression based on incident points.
- **Parent Communication Logs:** Record contact history, methods, and outcomes.
- **Real‑Time Analytics:** View behavior trends across grade levels and classes.
- **Export to CSV:** Export incident reports by student, class, or grade.
- **Counsellor Caseload Dashboard:** See all students in your purview with priority flags.

## 🔒 Privacy-First Security

School IT departments love TrackEDU because it operates on a strict **"Privacy by Design"** framework.

- **Zero PII Required:** Students use auto‑generated alphanumeric IDs (e.g., `EMWA482`) and Class Passcodes. No emails, passwords, or Google SSO needed.
- **Privacy‑First Architecture:** Student names are partially displayed (e.g., "Bill S."). No cross‑web tracking, no ads, no data selling.
- **Data Ownership:** Schools retain 100% ownership with ability to instantly wipe their database.
- **Encrypted Infrastructure:** Hosted on Google Cloud (Firebase) with AES‑256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
