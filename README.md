# 🏆 TrackEDU

**The Ultimate Digital School Culture & Gamification Platform**

TrackEDU is a premium Enterprise EdTech SaaS designed to replace clunky spreadsheets and manual tallies with a highly engaging, real-time House Points system. Built by former educators, it empowers teachers with frictionless classroom management tools while giving students a massive, RPG-style gamified experience—all without requiring any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

---

## ✨ Core Ecosystem

TrackEDU operates as a dual-sided ecosystem tailored for both screen-free environments and 1-to-1 device schools.

### 👩‍🏫 The Teacher Command Center
A frictionless, PWA-optimized dashboard for educators to drive school spirit.
* **Instant Point Awarding:** Award or deduct points to individuals, groups, or whole classes in seconds.
* **Classroom Utilities (Micro-Tools):** Includes a Magic Hat (random cold-calling), Squad Builder (instant group generation), and Magic Timer.
* **Live Projector Mode:** A beautiful, auto-rotating full-screen display for school assemblies that showcases live house standings and top contributing students.
* **Admin Dashboard:** CSV bulk imports, auto-balancing algorithms, and cross-classroom point syncing.

### 🚀 The Student Portal (Pro Ecosystem)
A full-blown gamified web app that students can access on their own devices.
* **The Learner's Journey (3D Binders):** As students earn points, they unlock 3D, holographic trading cards that evolve as they reach new milestones.
* **Daily Streaks & Hidden Gems:** Students check in daily to build streaks, unlock 5-day jackpot spins, and catch rare gems floating across their dashboard.
* **House Power Boosts (Reactors):** Students tap their daily "Boost" to charge their House Reactor. When it hits 100%, a "House Blast" awards bonus points to the entire team.
* **Classified Missions:** Multi-player "Mystery Word" puzzles where students receive a single secret letter and must collaborate across classrooms to crack the code.
* **Student Voice Polls:** Live, verified voting on school themes and events.

## 🔒 Bank-Grade Security & Compliance

School IT departments love TrackEDU because it operates on a **"Privacy by Design"** framework.

* **Zero PII Required:** Students do *not* need emails, passwords, or Google SSO to log in. They use an auto-generated alphanumeric ID (e.g., `EMWA482`) and a custom Class Passcode.
* **FERPA & GDPR Compliant:** We do not track students across the web, run ads, or sell data.
* **Data Sovereignty:** Schools retain 100% ownership of their data with the ability to permanently wipe their database via the dashboard instantly.
* **Encrypted Infrastructure:** Hosted on Google Cloud (Firebase) using AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

## 💼 SaaS Pricing Model

TrackEDU uses a Product-Led Growth (PLG) strategy with three distinct tiers:

1.  **Classroom Plan (Free Forever):** For the solo teacher. Max 50 students, 1 class. Manual entry and core classroom tools. No Student App access.
2.  **Core Campus Plan:** For schools wanting a unified, screen-free system. Unlimited students/teachers, CSV imports, and Live Projector Mode. 
3.  **Pro Ecosystem Plan:** The ultimate digital culture platform. Unlocks the Student App, live polls, 3D badge binders, and all advanced gamification features.
