TrackEDU is a **personal software development project** demonstrating a school culture operating system concept featuring three integrated demonstration tools: the **Teacher App**, **Student Portal**, and **Behavior Tracker**. Built by a solo developer (former educator), it showcases frictionless classroom tools, RPG-style gamification, and tiered behavior intervention tracking—all without requiring any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

> ⚠️ **Status:** TrackEDU is a personal development project in private testing phase. No company is registered. No commercial services are offered. This is a technical demonstration only.

---

## ✨ Core Ecosystem (Concept Demo)

TrackEDU operates as a unified platform concept with three integrated demonstration tools. Everything syncs in real‑time for testing purposes.

### 👩‍🏫 Teacher App (Demo)
A frictionless, PWA‑optimized dashboard concept for educators.
- **Instant Point Awarding:** Award or deduct points to individuals, groups, or whole classes.
- **12+ Classroom Utilities:** Magic Hat, Squad Builder, Focus Timer, Duo Mode, Role Roulette, Quick Quiz (250+ questions), and more.
- **Live Projector Mode:** Full‑screen display for assemblies showcasing live house standings.
- **Admin Dashboard:** CSV bulk imports, auto‑balancing algorithms, and cross‑classroom point syncing.

### 🚀 Student Portal (Demo)
A gamified web app concept that students access on their own devices.
- **The Learner's Journey (3D Binders):** 3D holographic trading cards that evolve across 30 tiers.
- **Daily Streaks & Mystery Spins:** Daily check‑ins to build streaks and unlock bonus point spins.
- **House Power Boosts (Reactors):** Students charge their House Reactor; at 100%, a "House Blast" awards bonus points.
- **Mystery Word Missions:** Multi‑player puzzles requiring collaboration across classrooms.
- **Student Voice Polls:** Live, verified voting on school themes and events.

### 📊 Behavior Tracker (Demo)
A dedicated tool concept for counsellors and school leaders.
- **Tiered Tracking (Pre‑Tier 1 to Tier 3):** Automatic progression based on incident points.
- **Parent Communication Logs:** Record contact history, methods, and outcomes.
- **Real‑Time Analytics:** View behavior trends across grade levels and classes.
- **Export to CSV:** Export incident reports by student, class, or grade.
- **Counsellor Caseload Dashboard:** See all students in your purview with priority flags.

## 🔒 Privacy-First Design

- **Zero PII Required:** Students use auto‑generated alphanumeric ID (e.g., `EMWA482`) and Class Passcode.
- **Privacy‑First Display:** Student names partially displayed (e.g., "Bill S." instead of full name).
- **Data Ownership:** Schools retain 100% ownership with ability to permanently wipe database.
- **Encrypted Infrastructure:** Google Cloud (Firebase) using AES‑256 encryption and TLS 1.3.
