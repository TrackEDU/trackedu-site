How to Use
A simple classroom calm challenge for students.
1 Choose an activity mode.

Use Silent Reading, Pair Work, Group Work, Presentation, or Exam Mode. This makes the meter fair for the lesson.

2 Set a calm goal.

Start with 1 minute. Increase the goal once the class understands the routine.

3 Press Start.

The tool checks sound level only. It does not save words or recordings.

4 Let students follow the big message.

Green says “Shhhhh...”, yellow says “Too loud...”, red says “Pause!”, and reward says “Great job!”.

5 Reward calm teamwork.

Use automatic rewards when the goal is reached, or press Reward when you want to recognise the class manually.

Tip: use Full Screen on a smartboard so students can focus on the mascot, message, timer and main controls.
