Frequently Asked Questions

Is this only for classrooms?

No. Schools can add classrooms, halls, labs, libraries, sports areas, auditoriums, meeting rooms and any other shared spaces.

Can some rooms require approval?

Yes. High-demand spaces can require administrator approval, while everyday spaces can be available for faster instant booking.

Can faculty and staff use it on mobile?

Yes. The facility portal is web-based and supports progressive web app installation, so staff can use it from phones, tablets and desktop devices.

Does it support recurring bookings?

Yes. Staff can make recurring block bookings up to 8 weeks out where the school allows them.

What makes TrackEDU different from using Google Calendar or spreadsheets?

TrackEDU Facilities is built around school-specific room booking workflows, including approval queues, shared-space visibility, QR check-in, auto-release options, recurring school bookings, site team setup requests and safer staff onboarding.

Who is this school facility booking system for?

It is designed for schools, colleges and universities that manage classrooms, halls, labs, libraries, sports facilities, meeting rooms, performance spaces and other shared campus resources.

TrackEDU Campus Room Booking & Simple Facility Calendar Features

TrackEDU Facilities is a simple, shared room booking calendar built specifically for schools, colleges, universities, and faculty. Our software stops classroom double-bookings, simplifies campus scheduling, and helps site teams stay organized.

Stop Double-Bookings with Live Shared Campus Calendars

Our live, real-time calendar prevents faculty from accidentally booking the same room. Users can instantly look up empty classrooms, check computer lab availability by period, and book open spaces securely from a phone or computer.

Close Rooms for Repairs & Rainy Day Field Closures

Administrators can easily block out rooms or labs for repairs and maintenance. If severe weather or heavy rain rolls in, administrators can pause all outdoor bookings with a single click to instantly alert staff and keep students safe.

Swap Rooms with Faculty & Send Setup Task Lists to the Site Team

Simplify staff communication with room-swap requests that let faculty securely trade schedules without emailing back and forth. Campus site teams and custodians get a clear daily list of tasks showing exactly where exam desks, chairs, or AV equipment need to be set up.
