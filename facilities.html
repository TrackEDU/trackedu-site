FAQ

Common questions

Is this only for classrooms?

No. Schools can add classrooms, halls, labs, libraries, sports areas, auditoriums, meeting rooms and any other shared spaces.

Can some rooms require approval?

Yes. High-demand spaces can require administrator approval, while everyday spaces can be available for faster booking.

Can teachers use it on mobile?

Yes. The facility portal is web-based and supports progressive web app installation, so it can work like an app on supported devices.

Does it support recurring bookings?

Yes. Staff can make recurring bookings where the school allows them, making regular room use easier to manage.

Can it be used without the full TrackEDU ecosystem?

Yes. TrackEDU Facilities can be bought as a standalone Facility Management System, or used as part of the full TrackEDU ecosystem.

TrackEDU School Room Booking & Facility Management System Features

TrackEDU Facilities is an educational enterprise scheduling platform built to streamline space utilization across K-12 and university campuses. Our software eliminates room scheduling conflicts and modernizes traditional school operations.

Automated School Conflict Prevention & Live Shared Calendars

Our real-time calendar tracking interface protects high-demand school environments from double bookings. Faculty can instantly query open periods, filter available classrooms by resources, and request instant space allocations securely.

School Maintenance Blocks & Severe Weather Facility Safety Protocol

Administrators can flag physical facilities for administrative closure during facility maintenance windows or emergencies. The emergency climate lock feature instantly closes outdoor fields, running tracks, and open-air courts across the entire ecosystem simultaneously during severe weather alerts.

Peer-to-Peer Room Swaps, Automated Approvals & Custodian Setup Task Logs

Simplify communication with native negotiation workflows that allow staff to trade room assignments without administrative oversight. Operations and site management teams can instantly render clean custodian schedule exports via structured CSV workflows to manage daily chair setups, technical AV preparation, and localized maintenance tickets effortlessly.
