System Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear operational logic for school administrators and finance leads.

How does the system calculate different week rotation cycles?
The core configuration engine adapts to your structural timetable setup parameters. School administrators can adjust cycle lengths (e.g., standard 5-day blocks or specific variations) and instruct the engine rules to skip Saturdays, Sundays, and public holiday logs automatically.
Can we restrict who can approve bookings for high-demand spaces?
Yes. When setting up your facility access entries list, you toggle whether a location requires admin approval. Standard rows (like typical classrooms) can be booked with 1 click instantly, while high-demand resources (like the main auditorium or swimming pool) automatically hold requests in a pending queue until authorized by designated staff.
How do maintenance blocks and holiday closures handle active bookings?
Saving an active maintenance blockade or custom holiday parameter will automatically flag any concurrent requests. Overlapping records are cascade-cancelled cleanly, and the system logs clear administrative notices (e.g., "Air Conditioning Repair") onto affected dashboards.
Does data sovereignty and export access remain under our school's control?
Absolutely. Administrators can compile and download clean, full CSV manifests of your entire school booking history, custodian shifts, and usage summaries at any time. Your database remains insulated, and full system wipe controls are accessible directly inside your secure profile dashboard.
