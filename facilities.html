FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Which TrackEDU Facilities plan should we start with?

Free Starter is best for testing the system with 1 bookable space and up to 10 users. Pro is designed for most schools, with up to 30 bookable spaces and up to 200 users. Ultimate is for larger schools, colleges, universities and multi-site groups that need custom capacity and implementation support.

Is this only for classrooms?

No. Schools can add classrooms, halls, labs, libraries, sports areas, auditoriums, meeting rooms and other shared spaces.

Do staff need another username and password?

No separate TrackEDU password is required when your organisation uses supported Google or Microsoft sign-in. Staff can use familiar school accounts, subject to your organisation’s access and approval settings.

Can some rooms require approval?

Yes. High-demand spaces can require administrator approval, while everyday spaces can be available for faster instant booking.

Can staff use it on mobile and tablet?

Yes. The facility portal is web-based and works across phones, tablets and desktop devices. It also supports progressive web app installation for quicker access.

Can rooms be blocked for maintenance, events or bad weather?

Yes. Administrators can close rooms or outdoor spaces for repairs, exams, events, holidays, severe weather or planned maintenance.

Can we try TrackEDU Facilities before purchasing?

Yes. New school workspaces include 90 days of full Pro access with up to 30 bookable spaces and 200 users. No credit card is required. After the trial, you can continue on Pro for USD 399/year or move to Free Starter.

TrackEDU Campus Room Booking and Simple Facility Calendar Features

TrackEDU Facilities is a simple, shared room booking calendar built specifically for schools, colleges, universities and faculty. Our software stops classroom double-bookings, simplifies campus scheduling and helps site teams stay organised.

Stop Double-Bookings with Live Shared Campus Calendars

Our live, real-time calendar prevents faculty from accidentally booking the same room. Users can instantly look up empty classrooms, check computer lab availability by period and book open spaces securely from a phone or computer.

Close Rooms for Repairs and Rainy Day Field Closures

Administrators can easily block out rooms or labs for repairs and maintenance. If severe weather or heavy rain rolls in, administrators can pause all outdoor bookings with a single click to instantly alert staff and keep students safe.

Swap Rooms with Faculty and Send Setup Task Lists to the Site Team

Simplify staff communication with room-swap requests that let faculty securely trade schedules without emailing back and forth. Campus site teams and custodians get a clear daily list of tasks showing exactly where exam desks, chairs or AV equipment need to be set up.
