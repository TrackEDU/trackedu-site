The real problem

Schools do not just need a booking calendar. They need operational confidence.

A spreadsheet can show a list. It cannot tell a teacher whether the lab is genuinely free, whether the hall needs approval, whether maintenance has blocked the room, or whether a recurring request will collide with another class three weeks later.

TrackEDU Facilities sells the outcome: fewer interruptions, clearer ownership, smoother staff routines, and a campus where shared spaces are easier to use because everyone works from the same live system.

Email chains create invisible decisions

Bookings get approved informally, forwarded, missed or buried, leaving teachers uncertain and administrators exposed.

Spreadsheets age the moment they are shared

Multiple copies, hidden edits and unclear ownership make it difficult to know which version is correct.

Teachers lose time chasing availability

The room finder and weekly grid make availability visible before staff commit to a lesson plan or event.

Operations teams need a setup view

Custodian exports convert bookings into a practical, chronological preparation list.

FAQ

Questions school leaders usually ask.

Clear answers for principals, operations managers, IT teams, finance leads and staff coordinators.

Can TrackEDU Facilities be used without the full TrackEDU platform?
Yes. It can be used as a standalone facilities booking system. Schools can later expand into the wider TrackEDU ecosystem if they want to connect facilities with school culture, rewards, house points and behaviour tracking.
How does it reduce double bookings?
The system centralises bookings in one live schedule, shows availability, records booking states and supports approval workflows for high-demand spaces. Recurring bookings and blocked spaces are also checked against availability rules.
Can different spaces have different approval rules?
Yes. A school can make ordinary rooms instantly bookable while requiring administrative approval for spaces such as auditoriums, pools, exam halls, specialist labs or sports facilities.
Does it support recurring bookings?
Yes. Staff can request recurring series, and administrators can require approval for recurring bookings to reduce longer-term timetable conflicts.
How does it help facilities, maintenance or custodian teams?
Maintenance blocks make unavailable spaces visible. Custodian CSV exports provide a chronological list of facilities, times, classes and notes, helping support teams prepare rooms and equipment more efficiently.
Can it be installed like an app?
Yes. The facilities tool includes progressive web app support, so staff can install it for faster access where their browser and device allow it.
How do schools pay?
Schools can request an invoice for annual licensing, which is often easier for institutional procurement than card payment. You can also contact hello@trackedu.net for procurement questions.
