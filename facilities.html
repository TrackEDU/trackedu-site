FAQ

Common questions

Short answers for school leaders and operations teams.

Can we use it as a standalone system?
Yes. TrackEDU Facilities works as a standalone Facility Management System for schools. You do not need to buy the full TrackEDU ecosystem to use it.
Can some spaces be instant-book and others approval-only?
Yes. Schools can choose which spaces are easy to book instantly and which need approval, such as auditoriums, specialist rooms or high-demand sports spaces.
Does it support recurring bookings?
Yes. Staff can create recurring bookings, which is helpful for regular lessons, clubs and repeating activities.
Does it help operations teams too?
Yes. The system includes maintenance blocks, clear booking states, notifications and export options that help operations and setup teams stay aligned.
