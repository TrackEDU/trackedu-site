FAQ

Simple answers for schools.

Can it be used without the full TrackEDU platform?

Yes. TrackEDU Facilities can run as a standalone Facility Management System.

What spaces can schools manage?

Rooms, halls, labs, libraries, sports facilities, fields, specialist spaces and any shared campus resource.

Can some spaces require approval?

Yes. Schools can decide which spaces are instantly bookable and which need administrator approval.

Does it support recurring bookings?

Yes. Staff can request repeated bookings for lessons, clubs, assemblies, rehearsals, exams or events.

Can teams export booking information?

Yes. Administrators can export CSV lists for records, setup planning and custodian workflows.
