Applying as a Founding School

Founding Schools are selected partner schools that receive 1 year of full access with the licence fee waived, in exchange for thoughtful early feedback on school workflows and implementation.

Include in your message

  • School name and country
  • Approximate student number
  • Current house, rewards, or behaviour systems
  • Who would lead the pilot internally

What happens next

  • TrackEDU reviews your school details.
  • We contact you to discuss fit and setup.
  • If selected, we confirm the licence waiver, onboarding, and support.
