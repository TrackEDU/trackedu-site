Frequently Asked Questions

Do students need an email address to log in?

No. We built TrackEDU specifically to avoid PII (Personally Identifiable Information) issues. Students access their Pro Ecosystem dashboard using an anonymous, auto-generated ID token (e.g., EMWA482) and a Class Passcode. It requires zero IT integration.

Can teachers see points awarded by other teachers?

Yes! In both our Core Campus and Pro Ecosystem plans, the data syncs across the entire school. If Mr. Smith awards 5 points in Math, Ms. Johnson will instantly see those points reflected in her Science class.

How do we get our student list into the system?

For school-wide plans, we provide a simple CSV template. You paste your student names, grades, and classes into the sheet, upload it, and our system automatically builds your entire database and auto-balances the houses in seconds.

Is our school data secure?

Absolutely. We run on highly secure, encrypted cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase). We do not run ads, we do not track student web data, and your school administrator can permanently wipe your entire database with a single click at any time.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

Your data is saved and waiting for you. Simply enter a credit card in the billing portal to continue. No data is held hostage — you can export everything anytime, even if you cancel.

What login options does TrackEDU support?

TrackEDU supports Google Workspace login via OAuth for teachers and staff. Students use a secure, anonymous ID token (e.g., EMWA482) combined with a Class Passcode — no email or password required. We do not currently integrate with SIS platforms like iSAMS, SIMS, or PowerSchool, but all data can be exported to CSV for import into your existing systems.

What happens to our data if we cancel?

Your data belongs to you. You can export all student points, history, and rosters to CSV anytime. If you cancel, we retain your data for 90 days. After that, it's permanently deleted unless you've exported it.

Why TrackEDU over ClassDojo?

ClassDojo is a classroom behavior tool. TrackEDU is a school-wide culture platform. With TrackEDU, every teacher sees the same live data, students get 3D badges and gamification, families have a public dashboard, and your house system finally works — without the admin. We're not a replacement for ClassDojo; we're the missing layer that connects your whole school.

What is a house point tracker and why do schools need one?

A house point tracker is a digital tool that records and displays points earned by students for their assigned houses. Schools need a reliable house point tracker to replace manual spreadsheets, ensure consistency across teachers, and keep students engaged. TrackEDU is the leading digital house point app that makes house systems work — with real-time leaderboards, student gamification, and zero administrative overhead.

Can TrackEDU be used for inter-house competitions and school events?

Absolutely! TrackEDU is designed specifically for inter-house competitions, sports days, academic challenges, and school-wide events. The Projector Mode displays live house rankings on any screen — perfect for assemblies and sports days. Teachers can award points instantly during events, and students can track their house's progress in real-time through the Student App (Pro plan).

How does TrackEDU help build school culture and community?

TrackEDU transforms school culture by making positive behavior visible and celebrated. Students see their contributions matter to their house. Parents can view live standings via the public dashboard link. The gamified Student App (Pro plan) creates daily engagement through streaks, badges, and power boosts. When students feel connected to their house, school spirit grows naturally — and TrackEDU gives you the tools to nurture that connection effortlessly.

Is TrackEDU suitable for primary schools, secondary schools, or both?

TrackEDU works brilliantly for both primary and secondary schools. Primary schools love the simple interface and visual leaderboards that students understand instantly. Secondary schools appreciate the robust gamification features (streaks, badges, power boosts) that keep older students engaged. Our flexible house system adapts to any school structure — whether you have 4 houses across 12 grades or 6 houses across primary-only. Many international schools use TrackEDU across both primary and secondary with seamless data sync.
