Frequently Asked Questions

Can you convert our existing Google Sheets into apps?

Yes. This is one of our most popular services. If you have a complex spreadsheet for tracking budgets, inventory, or student data, we can turn it into a secure, mobile-friendly WebApp. You keep the data, but lose the mess.

What other tools do you offer besides House Points?

We provide a suite of school tools including a Smart Fixture Tool (for sports teams), Facility Booking Manager (for room reservations), and Behaviour Trackers. All tools integrate seamlessly with Google Workspace.

Do students need to log in?

No. Our House Points system is designed to be "invisible" to students. Only teachers log in to award points. Students view live dashboards in the hallway or via a shared link.

Is our data secure?

Absolutely. Unlike other providers, TrackEDU operates on your school's own Google infrastructure. We do not store or sell student data. You retain full data sovereignty.

The Pilot Process

Everything you need to know about getting started

We know schools are busy.

A Zero-Friction approach to trying new tech.

No commitments. No heavy IT installs. Just clarity.

1

Discovery Call (30 mins)

A quick chat to see if we fit. We will cover:

  • Your current house structure (names, colors, milestones).
  • Your pain points (manual counting, lack of engagement).
  • Demo: We show you the admin backend live.
Q: "Do I need to bring my IT Director?"
No, this call is for House Leaders/SLT. We discuss tech requirements (which are minimal) later.
Q: "Is there a cost for the discovery?"
None. It is purely to see if TrackEDU solves your problem.
2

Custom Build (Done for You)

If you proceed, we build your system. You send us a student list, we do the rest.

  • We create your unique TrackEDU instance.
  • We import your students/classes.
  • We configure your branding.
Q: "How long does this take?"
Usually 24-48 hours after we get your student list.
Q: "Who has access to the data?"
During the pilot, we retain shared access to help you troubleshoot and update the system. This access is removed immediately if the pilot ends.
3

Launch & Support

We hand over the keys. You get a training video to share with staff (it takes 3 minutes to learn).

  • Direct WhatsApp/Email support for the lead teacher.
  • We help monitor the first week to ensure smooth running.
Q: "Does it work on iPads?"
Yes. It works on any device with a browser. No app store downloads needed.
Q: "How do teachers log in?"
They use their existing School Google Accounts. Single Sign-On (SSO) is built-in.

Common Questions from Schools

What happens after the pilot?

If you proceed, we move to a license. If not, we remove our access immediately. You keep the Google Sheet and all history. No lock-in.

Does it integrate with iSAMS/SIMS?

TrackEDU is designed to be lightweight and standalone to avoid complex IT integration. However, all data is exportable to CSV instantly.

Can parents see it?

Yes! We provide a secure, read-only "Community Link" that shows live scores and milestones without revealing sensitive student data.

Is it customizable?

100%. You define the House names, the Point categories (e.g., "Resilience", "Service"), and the Milestone awards.
