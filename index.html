Frequently Asked Questions

What are the three tools in TrackEDU?
TrackEDU includes three integrated applications: Teacher App (12+ classroom tools for awarding points, running assemblies, and managing classes), Student Portal (gamified engagement with 3D badges, streaks, and house power boosts), and Behavior Tracker (tiered interventions, parent communication logs, and real-time analytics for school leaders). All three share one database and sync in real-time.
Do I need an existing house system to use TrackEDU?
Not at all. TrackEDU works whether you have houses, teams, cohorts, or nothing at all. You can rename "Houses" to match your school's terminology in one click. Many schools start with just the Teacher App and Behavior Tracker, then add house features later.
Do students need an email address to log in?
No. We built TrackEDU specifically to avoid PII (Personally Identifiable Information) issues. Students access their Student Portal using an anonymous, auto-generated ID token (e.g., EMWA482) and a Class Passcode. Zero IT integration required.
Can teachers see behavior data from other teachers?
Yes, with appropriate permissions. TrackEDU provides role-based access. Homeroom teachers see data for their students. Counsellors (SAC) and school leaders (SLT) have broader visibility. All data syncs in real-time across the entire school.
How does the Behavior Tracker work with the Teacher App?
The Behavior Tracker pulls student data directly from your Teacher App roster. When a teacher logs an incident, it appears instantly in the Behavior Tracker dashboard. school leaders can add parent communication notes, track tier progression, and export reports — all without re-entering student information.
How do we get our student list into the system?
We provide a simple CSV template. You paste your student names, grades, classes, and house assignments (optional) into the sheet, upload it, and our system automatically builds your entire database in seconds. Auto-balance houses with one click if needed.
Is our school data secure?
Absolutely. We run on encrypted cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase) with AES-256 encryption and TLS 1.3. We do not run ads, we do not track student web data, and your school administrator can permanently wipe your entire database with a single click at any time. Region-specific hosting available for SG/UK.
What happens after the 30-day pilot?
After your pilot, you can either join our Founding Schools Program (limited to 5 schools — full license waiver for 1 year + training) or continue with a standard annual license. If TrackEDU isn't right for your school, you can export all your data to CSV and walk away. No data is held hostage.
What login options does TrackEDU support?
Teachers and staff use Google Workspace login via OAuth. Students use a secure, anonymous ID token combined with a Class Passcode — no email or password required. school leaders access the Behavior Tracker with their same Google credentials. No SIS integration required — all data imports via CSV.
What happens to our data if we cancel?
Your data belongs to you. You can export all student points, behavior incidents, and rosters to CSV anytime from the settings panel. If you cancel, we retain your data for 90 days. After that, it's permanently deleted unless you've exported it.
How does TrackEDU differ from classroom tools like ClassDojo?
ClassDojo excels at individual classroom management and teacher-parent communication. TrackEDU is a school-wide culture operating system with three integrated tools. Where classroom tools focus on a single teacher-student relationship, TrackEDU connects all grade levels through a unified system — giving teachers 12+ classroom tools, students a gamified portal, and school leaders tiered behavior tracking. Many schools use both: ClassDojo for daily classroom feedback and TrackEDU for school-wide culture, house systems, and behavior intervention tracking.
Can TrackEDU be used for inter-house competitions and school events?
Absolutely! The Teacher App includes Projector Mode for assemblies — displaying live house rankings on any screen. The Student Portal shows real-time leaderboards and house power boosts. Perfect for sports days, academic challenges, and weekly assemblies.
How does TrackEDU help with behavior intervention tracking?
The Behavior Tracker provides tiered intervention tracking from Pre-Tier 1 through Tier 3. school leaders can log parent communications, track follow-up actions, and view real-time analytics on behavior trends. Export incident reports by student, class, or grade level. All data syncs with the Teacher App so everyone stays informed.
Is TrackEDU suitable for primary schools, secondary schools, or both?
TrackEDU works brilliantly for both. Primary schools love the simple Teacher App interface and visual leaderboards. Secondary schools appreciate robust gamification (3D badges, streaks, power boosts) and the Behavior Tracker for tiered interventions. Our system adapts to any school structure from 50 to 3,000+ students.
What support do you provide during the pilot?
All pilot schools receive priority email support within 24 hours. Founding Schools receive dedicated implementation support, staff training workshops, and a direct contact person. We also provide video tutorials, setup guides, and a knowledge base for self-service help.
