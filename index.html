Chapter 9 / Questions Before You Start

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers for school leaders, teachers, and pastoral teams before starting a pilot.

Platform & Tools

(6 questions)
What are the three tools in TrackEDU?
TrackEDU includes three integrated applications: Teacher App for awarding points, running assemblies, and managing classes; Student Portal for gamified engagement through badges, streaks, and house progress; and Behavior Tracker for tiered interventions, parent communication logs, and real-time visibility for school leaders. All three tools share one connected database.
Do I need an existing house system to use TrackEDU?
No. TrackEDU works whether your school already uses houses, teams, cohorts, or is starting from scratch. Schools can keep their existing house names and structures, or create a new system during setup.
How does the Behavior Tracker work with the Teacher App?
The Behavior Tracker uses student information from the same school database as the Teacher App. When behaviour information is added, authorised staff can review patterns, add follow-up notes, track intervention stages, and export reports without re-entering student information.
How does TrackEDU differ from classroom tools like ClassDojo?
Classroom tools such as ClassDojo are often centred on individual classroom management and teacher-parent communication. TrackEDU is designed as a school-wide culture platform that connects the Teacher App, Student Portal, and Behavior Tracker through one system. It is intended for schools that want house points, student engagement, pastoral visibility, and behaviour support to work together.
Can TrackEDU be used for inter-house competitions and school events?
Yes. The Teacher App includes tools for assemblies and live displays, while the Student Portal gives students access to house progress, badges, streaks, and leaderboards. This makes TrackEDU suitable for sports days, academic challenges, weekly assemblies, and wider school culture initiatives.
Is TrackEDU suitable for primary schools, secondary schools, or both?
TrackEDU is designed to support both primary and secondary schools. Primary schools may benefit from simple point-awarding, visual leaderboards, and class engagement tools. Secondary schools may benefit from school-wide visibility, student engagement features, and structured behaviour support workflows.

School Setup & Data

(4 questions)
Do students need an email address to log in?
No. Students access the Student Portal using an auto-generated student ID token and a class passcode provided by the teacher. This means students do not need personal email addresses or passwords to use the portal.
How do we get our student list into the system?
TrackEDU uses a simple CSV import workflow. Schools can prepare student names, grades, classes, and house assignments in a spreadsheet, then upload them into the system. A setup demo is available on the site so school teams can see the import process in action.
What login options does TrackEDU support?
Teachers and staff use Google Workspace login. Students use an auto-generated student ID token combined with a class passcode, so they do not need email addresses or passwords. Student data can be imported through CSV during setup.
Can teachers see behaviour data from other teachers?
TrackEDU uses role-based access. Homeroom teachers can view information relevant to their students, while counsellors, pastoral teams, and school leaders can be given broader visibility where appropriate. Access can be managed according to your school's internal responsibilities.

Pricing, Privacy, Security & Billing

(11 questions)
How much does TrackEDU cost after the pilot?
TrackEDU offers a free 30-day school pilot. After the pilot, schools can continue with an early adopter annual licence from USD 3,000/year. Pricing may vary depending on school size, implementation needs, and support requirements. Selected Founding Schools may receive 1 year of full school access with the licence fee waived.
What happens after the 30-day pilot?
After the pilot, your school can decide whether TrackEDU is a good fit. Schools can continue with an early adopter annual licence from USD 3,000/year, or apply for the Founding Schools Programme if eligible. The Founding Schools Programme is limited to 5 selected schools worldwide and includes a 1-year licence fee waiver, training, and implementation support.
What is the difference between the 30-day pilot and the Founding Schools Programme?
The 30-day pilot allows any school to try TrackEDU before making a purchasing decision. The Founding Schools Programme is a separate limited programme for 5 selected schools worldwide. Selected Founding Schools receive 1 year of full access with the licence fee waived, plus additional training and implementation support.
Can my school pay for TrackEDU after the pilot?
Yes. Schools that wish to continue after the pilot can request an invoice for an early adopter annual school licence. Payment can be made by bank transfer, and a secure card payment link can be requested where needed. TrackEDU does not currently use automatic renewal.
What happens if we are not selected as a Founding School?
Schools that are not selected for the Founding Schools Programme can still continue with TrackEDU through an early adopter annual licence. Early adopter licences start from USD 3,000/year and can be arranged by invoice after the pilot period.
How does the annual school licence work?
Annual school access is activated with a secure licence code. Each code unlocks TrackEDU for the agreed licence period, usually 12 months. At the end of the licence period, access expires unless the school renews for a further year. Renewal is handled manually so schools can review usage, update billing details, and decide whether to continue.
Can our school pay by invoice, bank transfer, or card?
Yes. Schools can request an invoice by submitting their billing details through the invoice request form. TrackEDU will review the request and email the official invoice manually. Bank transfer details will be included once the invoice is issued. Schools that prefer to pay by card can request a secure card payment link.
When do we receive our licence activation code?
For paid school licences, the 1-year licence activation code is issued once payment is confirmed. For selected Founding Schools, the 1-year licence activation code is issued after approval into the programme. Licence activation is currently reviewed manually so that school details, access dates, and billing information are correct.
Do annual school licences auto-renew?
No. Annual school licences do not auto-renew. At the end of the licence period, access expires unless the school chooses to renew for another year. Renewal is handled manually so schools can review usage, billing details, and whether they want to continue.
Is our school data secure?
TrackEDU is built on secure Google Firebase infrastructure with protected database access and encrypted data transmission. We do not run ads, we do not sell student data, and we do not track students across the web. School administrators can export their data and use account deletion controls if they choose to leave TrackEDU.
What happens to our data if we leave TrackEDU?
Your school retains control of its data. Administrators can export student points, behaviour records, and rosters to CSV from the settings panel. If your school leaves TrackEDU, account and data deletion controls are available, and any retention period will follow the terms agreed with your school.

How does TrackEDU help with behaviour intervention tracking?

How to Create a Class

Add classes & bulk import students

Step 1: Initial System Setup

1. Go to House Control → Start Here. Upload your school logo and set the school name.

2. In House Control, click Configure Houses. Add names, colors, emojis, and mottos.

3. Go to Start Here → Import Students. Download the template, fill it out, and upload. IDs are auto‑generated!

4. In Start Here, use the Bulk Balance tool to instantly sort students into houses.

Step 2: How to Award Points

Quick Click: Go to Student Management. Tap any student's avatar to give +1 point. Long‑press to deduct.

Bulk Selection: Click Select Students. Tick multiple students, then use the popup toolbar to award points.

Whole Class/House: Click the orange Whole School Award button. Give points Per Student or Split Evenly.

Made a mistake? Go to Award History in the sidebar and click Undo.

Step 3: The Teacher Toolkit (12+ Tools)

🎩 Magic Hat: Select a class, spin the wheel, and randomly call on a student. Award points instantly!

👥 Group Builder: Instantly generate randomized groups for projects – mix by house or totally random.

🧠 Quick Quiz: Load pre‑made SEL/Values questions onto your projector to spark discussions.

🎟️ Class code: Generate a secret code. Students who type it into their app earn points!

⏱️ Focus Timer: Set a countdown timer for activities. Keep lessons on track with visual time reminders.

🏠 House Points: Award points to entire houses with one click. Perfect for assembly shout‑outs.

📊 Live Leaderboard: Display house standings on your smartboard. Updates in real time.

🎲 Random Student Picker: Another way to randomly select students – keeps everyone on their toes!

📝 Exit Tickets: Quick reflection questions at the end of class. Award points for thoughtful answers.

🏆 Class Awards: Create custom awards for your class (e.g., "Kindness Champion", "Math Master").

🔊 Class Calm Meter: Interactive noise meter – helps students self‑regulate volume levels.

📜 Certificate Studio: Generate beautiful printable certificates for students. Batch mode for end‑of‑term.

Plus: Assembly Mode, Student Voice Polls, Mystery Word, and House Power Boosts!

Behavior Tracker

Log, monitor, and improve student behavior across your school with tiered interventions.

Log Incidents

Use quick presets to log incidents in seconds. Add details, severity level, and action taken.

Track Patterns

See which times, days, or activities have the most incidents. Spot trends before they become problems.

Auto Escalation

Incidents move through 4 tiers. The right people (Teacher, SAC, SLT) are notified automatically.

Manage Tasks

Track follow‑ups like parent meetings or student reflections. Mark complete when done.

Example

A teacher logs a low‑level disruption. After 4 similar incidents, the system escalates to Tier 1 and notifies the Homeroom Teacher to contact parents.

Understanding the Tier System

Pre-Tier 1: Classroom‑managed issues. Verbal reminders, seat changes. Needs 4 points to progress.

Tier 1: Homeroom Teacher intervention. Parent contact may be needed. Needs 3 points.

Tier 2: Involves SAC (Student Affairs Coordinator) and BIP. Needs 2 points.

Tier 3: SLT (Senior Leadership Team) and BSP required. Needs 2 points to maintain.

Severity points: Low = 0.5 | Medium = 1 | High = 2

Notifications & Tasks

Click the Tasks button to see pending follow‑ups: 🔴 High Priority (parent communication) or 🟡 Medium Priority (student reflection).

Click "Edit Incident" to update and mark complete.
