Quick answers

Questions schools often ask.

The essentials at a glance. Find full product details, pricing and FAQs on the House Points page.

Is TrackEDU a House Points tracker for a whole school?

Yes. TrackEDU is an online House Points system for school-wide use, with staff awarding, live House leaderboards, student recognition, a Student Portal and leadership reporting. Schools can use their own House names and colours.

Can we import our existing classes?

Yes. TrackEDU supports CSV import and Google Classroom connections. Wonde MIS/SIS integration is live for paid subscribers, while pilot schools can explore Wonde in demo mode.

Does TrackEDU offer free school training?

Yes. We provide free professional development for school leaders, staff and House Leaders. Specialist House Leader training focuses on student and staff buy-in, House identity, student leadership and school culture.

What happens after the free 30-day pilot?

The pilot requires no credit card and does not automatically become paid. Schools choose whether to subscribe. Paid Stripe subscriptions renew annually unless cancelled, and you can cancel anytime to prevent the next renewal.
