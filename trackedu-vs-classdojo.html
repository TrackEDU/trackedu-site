FAQ

Questions about TrackEDU vs ClassDojo

A practical guide for school leaders, pastoral teams and teachers comparing their options.

Is TrackEDU the same as ClassDojo?
No. ClassDojo is widely known for classroom communication, teacher-family connection and classroom culture. TrackEDU is designed as a school-wide culture platform that connects house points, rewards, student engagement, behaviour tracking and pastoral visibility in one system.
Is TrackEDU a ClassDojo alternative?
TrackEDU can be considered an alternative for schools that need a school-wide platform for house points, behaviour tracking, rewards and pastoral visibility. It is not designed simply to replace family messaging tools. It is designed for schools that want points, behaviour and culture data connected across the whole school.
Which is better for house points, TrackEDU or ClassDojo?
TrackEDU is built specifically around school-wide house points, live leaderboards, student rewards, student portals and house identity. Schools with established or emerging house systems may find TrackEDU better suited to whole-school rewards and inter-house culture.
Does TrackEDU include behaviour tracking?
Yes. TrackEDU includes a Behavior Tracker for logging incidents, reviewing patterns, managing tiered interventions, recording parent communication and supporting pastoral follow-up.
Can TrackEDU replace spreadsheets for house points?
Yes. TrackEDU is designed to reduce manual house point tracking by giving schools live leaderboards, student records, house totals, point histories and school-controlled exports.
Can individual teachers use TrackEDU?
Yes. Individual teachers can start with the free Teacher Starter option for one class. Schools can also begin with a 30-day pilot if they want to test TrackEDU across a wider group of staff and students.
Can international schools use TrackEDU?
Yes. TrackEDU is designed for international schools that need flexible house systems, school-wide rewards, behaviour tracking, student engagement and staff visibility across classes, houses and pastoral teams.
Is TrackEDU affiliated with ClassDojo?
No. TrackEDU is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by ClassDojo. This page is an independent comparison intended to help schools understand which type of platform may better fit their needs.
