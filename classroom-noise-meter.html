How we protect your classroom

The Class Calm Meter is designed to be 100% safe for classroom use. Because this is a free, standalone tool, it operates differently from our main TrackEDU platform.

Microphone & Audio (Zero Recording)

To measure sound levels, your browser will ask for microphone access. We never record, listen to, or transmit classroom audio. The sound levels are processed mathematically in real-time directly on your computer's browser, and the audio data is instantly discarded.

Data Storage (100% Local)

This tool does not connect to a database or a server. Any settings you change or points you track are saved locally in your own browser's cache. If you clear your browser history, the data disappears.

Student Privacy (No Names Collected)

We do not collect student names, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information (PII) to use this free tool.
