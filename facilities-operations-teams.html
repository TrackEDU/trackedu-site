The real pain

Operations teams feel the impact of unclear bookings first.

When staff book spaces without setup details, maintenance visibility or approval rules, the problem lands with operations, site teams and front desk staff.

Missed setup details

Rooms are booked, but the people preparing them may not know about chairs, tables, AV, access or timing.

Maintenance is invisible

Rooms under repair or cleaning can still be requested if closures are not clearly visible to staff.

Too many last-minute questions

Reception, admin and site teams are asked who booked a room, whether it is free or why it is unavailable.

How it helps

Turn bookings into operational visibility.

The system helps operations teams move beyond a calendar by making setup needs, closures, approvals and space pressure easier to see.

Setup requests in the booking

Staff can add notes for chairs, desks, AV, cleaning, access or layout requirements when they book.

Clear closures and blocks

Operations can block spaces for maintenance, cleaning, exams, events or restricted access.

Leadership visibility

Simple usage views help leaders understand demand, pressure points and underused spaces.

Frequently asked questions

Can operations teams block rooms for maintenance?

Yes. Spaces can be marked unavailable for maintenance, cleaning, repairs, exams or other closures.

Can staff add setup requests when booking?

Yes. Booking notes can capture setup needs such as chairs, tables, AV, access, equipment or room layout.

Can some spaces require approval?

Yes. High-demand, restricted or specialist spaces can require approval before the booking is confirmed.

Can one department test it first?

Yes. Operations teams can start with a focused set of shared spaces, test the booking flow, and expand once staff see the value.
