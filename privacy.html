Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

TrackEDU ("we", "our", or "us") provides a complete school culture platform featuring three integrated tools: the Teacher App, Student Portal, and Behavior Tracker. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and security of educators, administrators, counsellors, and students.

Important Note: TrackEDU is currently in pre-launch / pilot phase. We are not yet a registered company. This Privacy Policy explains our current data practices during this pilot phase.

In the context of data protection laws, the School or Educational Institution acts as the Data Controller, and TrackEDU acts as the Data Processor. We only process student data under the direct instruction and authorization of the school.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization, collecting only what is strictly necessary to operate the three integrated tools.

A. Teacher & Administrator Data (Teacher App)

  • Account Information: First and last name, school email address, school affiliation, and professional role.
  • Authentication Data: We use Google Single Sign-On (SSO) to authenticate educators. We do not process or store your Google password.
  • Platform Activity: Points awarded, class management actions, and toolkit usage patterns.

B. Student Data (Student Portal & Behavior Tracker)

  • Profile Information: First name, last initial (or last name), grade/category, class assignment, and house assignment. Student names are partially displayed (e.g., "Bill S." instead of full name) to protect individual privacy.
  • Student Portal Activity: Points earned, 3D badges unlocked, daily streaks, house power boost contributions, poll participation, and mystery word mission progress.
  • Behavior Tracker Data: Incident logs, behavior types, severity levels, tier progression (Pre-Tier 1 to Tier 3), action taken, follow-up notes, and parent communication records.
  • What we DO NOT collect: We strictly prohibit and do not collect student email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, dates of birth, social security numbers, or sensitive demographic/health data. Students access the Student Portal via auto-generated anonymous ID tokens and shared class passcodes.

C. Automatically Collected Usage Data

  • Log data, device types, browser types, and non-identifying interaction metrics used strictly to monitor system security and ensure platform uptime.

3. How We Use the Information

We use the collected data exclusively to provide and support the three TrackEDU tools. Specifically, to:

  • Teacher App: Operate house point tracking, classroom toolkits (Magic Hat, Timer, Squad Builder), and live assembly displays.
  • Student Portal: Power gamification features including 3D badges, daily streaks, house power boosts, and mystery word missions.
  • Behavior Tracker: Enable tiered intervention tracking, parent communication logs, and basic behavior analytics for counsellors and school leaders.
  • Authenticate authorized school staff and counsellors.
  • Provide customer support and respond to technical issues.
Strict Prohibition: We never sell student or teacher data. We never use student data to target advertisements, build commercial profiles, or market third-party products.

4. Subprocessors & Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to provide the service through trusted infrastructure partners. Our partners are bound to privacy and security standards:

  • Google Cloud Platform (Firebase): Used for secure database hosting, real-time syncing across all three tools, and educator authentication.
  • Email Service: Used to route essential emails (e.g., account-related notifications) to educators and counsellors.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

TrackEDU does not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

We use browser Local Storage strictly for functional purposes within the Student Portal and Behavior Tracker, such as tracking "daily login streaks," preventing duplicate poll votes, temporarily locking gamification elements, and caching user preferences. This data remains on the device and is not used to track students across the internet.

6. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect educational records:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls: Database security rules strictly govern access, ensuring that student data is only accessible to authorized educators within that specific school domain.
  • Anonymous IDs: The Student Portal requires only a randomly generated alphanumeric ID and a shared Class Passcode.
  • Privacy-First Display: Student names are partially displayed (e.g., "Bill S." instead of full name) to protect individual privacy.
  • Role-Based Access: Homeroom Teachers see their students only. Counsellors see school-wide behavior data. School leaders have administrative visibility.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Schools have control over their data across all three tools.

  • During Pilot: Data is retained while your school actively uses the Platform.
  • Self-Serve Deletion: School Administrators have access to a "Full System Wipe" feature in their dashboard, allowing them to permanently delete all student records, points, behavior incidents, and history instantly.
  • Behavior Tracker Export: Counsellors can export all behavior incident reports to CSV before deletion.
  • Upon Request: If you wish to have your school's data deleted, please contact us. We will permanently delete all data within 30 days of your request.

8. Privacy-First Design Principles

FERPA Considerations (United States)

TrackEDU is designed with privacy-first principles to support schools in their educational record-keeping. We operate under the direct control of the school regarding the use and maintenance of educational records. Schools should consult their legal counsel to determine compliance with specific regulations.

COPPA Considerations (United States)

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) protects children under 13. TrackEDU relies on the School to provide verifiable consent to create student profiles. We intentionally do not collect PII directly from students.

GDPR Principles (European Union / UK)

Schools act as the Data Controller and TrackEDU as the Data Processor. Data processing is based on the legitimate interest of the school to administer educational programs. Schools should consult their legal counsel for GDPR compliance requirements.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you or your school administrators have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold.
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate data be updated.
  • Deletion: Request the erasure of your data (Right to be Forgotten).
  • Portability: Export data via the built-in CSV export tools in the Admin Dashboard.

Note: Parents or students wishing to exercise these rights must contact their School Administrator directly, as the school controls the data. We will assist the school in fulfilling these requests.
