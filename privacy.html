Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Overview

TrackEDU ("we", "our") respects your privacy and takes data protection seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled on the TrackEDU marketing website (trackedu.net) and during school-based pilots.

TrackEDU is operated by TrackEDU LLC (United States). We align our practices with international data protection standards and principles.

2. Data Collected on This Website

This marketing website collects limited information only.

Voluntary Information

When you submit an enquiry, request a demo, or join a pilot, we may collect:

  • Name
  • School email address
  • School name

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry, arrange demos, or communicate about TrackEDU services.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage (e.g. number of visitors, device type, approximate location).

This data is Anonymous, Aggregated, and Not used to personally identify visitors.

3. Student & School Data (Important)

Marketing Website

This website (trackedu.net) does not collect, store, or process any student data.

4. Data Handling During School Pilots

During a TrackEDU pilot, the TrackEDU application is deployed and managed by TrackEDU within a Google Workspace environment created specifically for the pilot.

Access & Control (During Pilot)

  • TrackEDU retains administrative ownership of the pilot system during the pilot period in order to provide setup, support, and technical assistance.
  • Schools are granted access to use the system for evaluation purposes.

End of Pilot Lifecycle

At the conclusion of the pilot period:

  • If the school continues: Ownership and administrative control are transferred fully to the school, and TrackEDU administrative access is removed.
  • If the school does not continue: The pilot system is reset or removed, and no data is retained by TrackEDU.

5. No External Data Sharing

TrackEDU does not:

  • Sell data
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use advertising trackers
  • Use external databases

All operational data stays within the Google Workspace environment.
