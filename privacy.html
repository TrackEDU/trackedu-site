Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Overview

TrackEDU ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data on our marketing website (trackedu.net). We are based in Singapore and align our practices with the PDPA.

2. Data We Collect

  • Voluntary Information: When you submit an enquiry or request a demo, we collect your Name, School Email, and School Name. We use this solely to contact you regarding TrackEDU services.
  • Analytics (Cookies): We use Google Analytics 4 to understand website traffic (e.g., how many visitors we get). This collects anonymous data like device type and approximate location. It does not identify you personally.

3. Student Data

Important:

This marketing website does NOT track or store any student data. The TrackEDU House Points application runs separately on your school's own Google Workspace infrastructure, ensuring data sovereignty.
