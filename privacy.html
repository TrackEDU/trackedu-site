Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Overview

TrackEDU ("we", "our") respects your privacy and takes data protection seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled on the TrackEDU marketing website (trackedu.net) and during school-based pilots.

TrackEDU is based in Singapore and aligns its practices with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

2. Data Collected on This Website

This marketing website collects limited information only.

Voluntary Information

When you submit an enquiry, request a demo, or join a pilot, we may collect:

  • Name
  • School email address
  • School name

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry, arrange demos, or communicate about TrackEDU services.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage (e.g. number of visitors, device type, approximate location).

This data is Anonymous, Aggregated, and Not used to personally identify visitors.

3. Student & School Data (Important)

Marketing Website

This website (trackedu.net) does not collect, store, or process any student data.

4. Data Handling During School Pilots

When a school participates in a TrackEDU pilot:

  • The TrackEDU application is deployed inside the school’s own Google Workspace.
  • All student and school data remains owned and controlled by the school.
  • Data is not stored on external servers.

Support Access During Pilot

During the pilot period only:

  • TrackEDU may be granted temporary access by the school to provide setup support and troubleshooting.
  • Access is limited to what is necessary for support purposes.

After the Pilot

At the end of the pilot:

  • If continuing: TrackEDU access is removed, and the system runs fully under the school’s control.
  • If stopping: The pilot system is reset or removed, and no data is retained by TrackEDU.

TrackEDU does not retain copies of student data after pilot access ends.

5. No External Data Sharing

TrackEDU does not:

  • Sell data
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use advertising trackers
  • Use external databases

All operational data stays within the school’s Google environment.
