Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

TrackEDU ("we", "our", or "us") provides a school culture platform featuring three integrated tools: the Teacher App, Student Portal, and Behavior Tracker. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of educators, administrators, counsellors, school leaders, and students.

Important note: TrackEDU is currently in pre-launch / pilot phase. We are in the process of formalising the business and are not yet operating as a fully registered company. This Privacy Policy explains our current data practices during this pilot phase.

For school-managed use of TrackEDU, the school or educational institution will generally act as the Data Controller, and TrackEDU will act as the Data Processor. This means that schools decide how TrackEDU is used with their staff and students, while TrackEDU processes data to provide the Platform. Specific legal roles may vary depending on jurisdiction and any future agreement with the school.

2. Information We Collect

We follow a data minimisation approach and aim to collect only the information needed to operate the Platform safely and effectively.

A. Teacher, Administrator & Counsellor Data

  • Account information: First and last name, school email address, school affiliation, and professional role.
  • Authentication data: We use Google sign-in to authenticate educators and school staff. We do not process or store your Google password.
  • Platform activity: Points awarded, class management actions, toolkit usage, and administrative actions needed to operate the Platform.

B. Student Data

  • Profile information: Student first name, last initial or last name where provided by the school, grade or category, class assignment, house assignment, and other school-defined grouping information.
  • Student Portal activity: Points earned, badges unlocked, streaks, house contributions, poll participation, and mission progress.
  • Behavior Tracker data: Incident logs, behaviour types, severity levels, tier progression, actions taken, follow-up notes, and parent communication records entered by authorised school staff.
  • Display minimisation: Where possible, student names are partially displayed, for example “Bill S.” rather than a full name, to reduce unnecessary exposure of student information.
Student data we do not require: TrackEDU does not require student email addresses, student passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, dates of birth, national identity numbers, or sensitive demographic data for students to access the Student Portal. Students use auto-generated ID tokens and class passcodes provided by their school.

C. Usage and Technical Data

  • Basic log data, device type, browser type, and non-identifying interaction metrics used to monitor platform performance, resolve technical issues, and support system security.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide, maintain, and support TrackEDU. Specifically, we use data to:

  • Teacher App: Operate house point tracking, classroom tools, class management, and live displays.
  • Student Portal: Power student-facing features including badges, streaks, house progress, polls, and missions.
  • Behavior Tracker: Enable tiered intervention tracking, parent communication logs, follow-up notes, and basic behaviour analytics for authorised school staff.
  • Authenticate authorised teachers, counsellors, administrators, and school leaders.
  • Provide technical support, respond to issues, and improve platform reliability.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.
Strict prohibition: We do not sell student or teacher data. We do not use student data to target advertisements, build commercial profiles, or market third-party products.

4. Subprocessors & Data Sharing

We do not share school data with third parties except where necessary to provide the Platform, maintain security, or operate essential services.

  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase: Used for database hosting, authentication, real-time syncing, and platform infrastructure.
  • Email and communication services: Used only to route essential account-related, support, or school communication messages.
  • Demo booking tools: Where used, third-party booking services may process information entered by staff when booking a demo or support call.

We do not permit subprocessors to use school or student data for advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

TrackEDU does not use third-party advertising cookies or advertising pixels.

We may use browser local storage and similar functional technologies to support features such as daily login streaks, preventing duplicate poll votes, saving user preferences, temporarily locking gamification elements, and maintaining session functionality. These technologies are used for platform functionality and are not used to track students across the internet.

6. Data Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect school and student data. These include:

  • Encrypted infrastructure: Data is protected through encrypted cloud infrastructure and secure transmission methods provided by our hosting and authentication providers.
  • Access controls: Database access is restricted so that school data is only available to authorised users within the relevant school environment.
  • Student access minimisation: Students use auto-generated ID tokens and class passcodes rather than email accounts or passwords.
  • Role-based access: Different users have different levels of visibility depending on their school-defined role. For example, teachers may see their own classes, counsellors may see behaviour support data, and school leaders may have broader administrative visibility.
  • Reduced display of student names: Where possible, student names are partially displayed to reduce unnecessary visibility.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We continue to review and improve our safeguards as TrackEDU develops.

7. Data Retention, Export & Deletion

Schools retain control over the data they enter into TrackEDU.

  • During pilot: School data is retained while the school actively uses the Platform during the pilot phase.
  • Export: Authorised school users may export relevant data, such as rosters, point records, and behaviour records, using available CSV export tools.
  • Deletion controls: School administrators may have access to account deletion or data deletion tools, including school-level deletion options where available.
  • Deletion requests: Schools may contact us to request deletion of their data. We will process verified deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, normally within 30 days, subject to technical, legal, and security requirements.
  • Backups and logs: Some residual copies may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, or audit records before being deleted according to our technical processes.

8. Privacy-First Design Principles

United States Education Privacy Considerations

TrackEDU is designed with privacy-first principles to support schools in managing educational records and school culture data. Schools remain responsible for determining how TrackEDU fits within their own legal, policy, and parental consent requirements.

Children’s Privacy Considerations

TrackEDU does not provide open student sign-up and does not require students to submit email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, or dates of birth. Student access is managed by schools using generated tokens and class passcodes.

UK / EU Data Protection Principles

For school-managed use, schools will generally act as Data Controllers and TrackEDU as Data Processor. TrackEDU is designed to support data minimisation, access control, export, and deletion. Schools should seek their own legal advice regarding their obligations under applicable data protection laws.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, authorised school users, parents, or students may have rights in relation to personal data, including:

  • Access: Requesting a copy of relevant personal data.
  • Correction: Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: Requesting deletion of data where legally applicable.
  • Portability: Exporting data through available CSV export tools.
  • Restriction or objection: Requesting limits on certain processing where applicable.

Parents or students wishing to exercise these rights should normally contact their school administrator first, as the school controls how TrackEDU is used. We will assist schools in responding to verified requests where appropriate.

10. Data Processing Agreements

For school deployments, TrackEDU may provide or enter into a Data Processing Agreement where required. This may set out additional details regarding processing instructions, subprocessors, retention, deletion, security, and school responsibilities.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TrackEDU develops, as our business structure changes, or as legal requirements evolve. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date and, where appropriate, notify active schools or users.
