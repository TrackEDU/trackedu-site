The real pain

PE spaces are high-demand, fast-moving and easy to clash.

PE teams manage lessons, fixtures, clubs, weather changes and shared spaces. When bookings happen through email, WhatsApp or memory, staff lose time and spaces get double-booked.

Double-booked spaces

Sports halls, gyms and fields are often shared across lessons, clubs, fixtures and events, which makes informal booking risky.

Weather changes

Outdoor spaces can become unusable quickly, but staff still need to know which backup spaces are available.

Fixture pressure

After-school fixtures, CCAs and tournaments need clear visibility, not last-minute messages across several staff groups.

How it helps

Give PE staff one place to check, book and plan.

Instead of chasing availability through messages, PE staff can open one calendar, find the right space and make a booking with the details everyone needs.

Clear space availability

See sports halls, gyms, fields, courts and shared PE spaces in one calendar, by day, time and booking status.

Faster booking decisions

Staff can quickly check what is free, book suitable spaces and reduce the time spent asking around.

Closures and restrictions

Mark outdoor areas as unavailable during weather closures, maintenance or events, so staff do not plan around unusable spaces.

Frequently asked questions

Can a PE department use the free plan?

Yes. The free starter plan includes up to 5 facilities and 15 users, which is enough for many PE teams to test sports halls, gyms, fields or courts.

Can outdoor spaces be closed during bad weather?

Yes. Outdoor spaces can be blocked or marked unavailable so staff do not accidentally book unusable areas.

Can PE staff use it on mobile?

Yes. TrackEDU Facilities is web-based and works on phones, tablets and computers.

Can this expand to the whole school later?

Yes. A PE department can start small, then the same system can be expanded to libraries, classrooms, halls, labs and operations teams.
